Questions, answered

Frequently Asked Questions.

What is DigX Voice Bot?

DigX Voice Bot is an AI voice-agent concept from ICCS DigX, designed to support natural phone conversations for customer enquiries, reminders, lead qualification and service workflows. Final capabilities depend on the configured solution.

How is it different from a traditional IVR?

A conversational voice agent is designed to understand spoken requests and respond in context, rather than relying only on fixed keypad menus. Available actions depend on connected systems and approved workflows.

Will customers know they are speaking to AI?

Customer-facing experiences should be transparent about the use of an automated assistant and provide an appropriate path to human support.

What happens if the bot cannot help?

A solution can be designed to route selected conversations to a human team, with relevant context passed along where the integration supports it.

Which languages are supported?

Language and voice availability must be confirmed for the specific product configuration and deployment.

How long does implementation take?

Timelines depend on use cases, integrations, security reviews, testing and deployment requirements. ICCS can confirm a realistic plan after discovery.

Can we try it before committing?

Yes. Contact ICCS to discuss a product demonstration and whether a pilot is suitable for your use case.
